Seoul

A city of street foods

Seoul, South Korea’s vibrant capital, is a captivating metropolis of stark contrasts where ancient Joseon-era palaces stand alongside neon-lit skyscrapers. Divided by the iconic Han River into the historic northern half of Gangbuk and the glitzy, modern southern half of Gangnam, this sprawling city feels less like a single destination and more like an exciting collection of distinct neighborhoods. From the serene Buddhist temples tucked away in urban green spaces to the hyper-modern digital technology defining everyday life, Seoul seamlessly bridges centuries of deep-rooted tradition and trend-setting contemporary culture.

For travelers, the city offers an endless array of unique pockets to explore depending on your style. You can wander through the beautiful traditional houses of Bukchon, shop the bustling markets of Myeongdong, or dive into the high-energy student nightlife and street food scenes of Hongdae and Sinchon. Whether you are looking to immerse yourself in history at Gyeongbokgung Palace, eat fresh seafood at the massive Noryangjin Fish Market, or experience the upscale luxury of the Gangnam district, Seoul promises a dynamic and unforgettable adventure around every corner.

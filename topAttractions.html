Gyeongbokgung Palace
The grandest and most historically significant of the Five Grand Palaces built during the Joseon Dynasty. It serves as an iconic symbol of Korean royal history, traditional architecture, and national resilience.

Bukchon Hanok Village
Historic Seoul neighborhood featuring hundreds of traditional, clay-tiled wooden houses. Once home to Joseon Dynasty nobles, it beautifully blends 600 years of history with modern city views.

Gwangjang Market
One of Seoul’s oldest traditional markets, universally famous for its chaotic food alleys. It serves legendary street delicacies like crispy mung bean pancakes and addictive bite-sized kimbap.
